Dark pine and fir trees, blueberries, thistle, moss-covered boulders, and challenging enemies. The Black Forest biome offers plenty of content to keep you coming back.

This biome unlocks most of the major systems in the game: mining, smelting, farming, fermenting, portals. It also introduces dungeons, dangerous swams of mobs, with some able to one-shot you dead.

At night, the encounters are more frequent and visibility is reduced because of the darkness.

## Overview

* Forage for [Blueberries]@ and [CarrotSeeds]@, and collect every [GreydwarfEye]@
* Scout for stone buildings, burial chambers, copper ore and tin
* Clear a burial chamber for **Surtling Cores**
* A **Charcoal Kiln** is [Stone](20) and [SurtlingCore](5); a **Smelter** costs the same
* Mine [CopperOre]@ from the big deposits and [TinOre]@ from the small shore ones
* Smelt copper, then build a **Forge** — [Stone](4), [Coal](4), [Wood](10), and [Copper](6)
* [Bronze]@ is an alloy of [Copper](2) and [Tin](1) made at the forge
* Craft a [AxeBronze]@ and return to the Meadows for [FineWood]@
* Establish a **Portal** at base and bring one with you for exploring
* Get some serious armor by defeating either a [TrophyFrostTroll]@"Troll" or a [TrophyBjorn]@"Bear"
* Build a **Cauldron** ([Tin](10)) to unlock cooking
* Build a **Mead Ketill** ([Tin](4), [Copper](6), [LeatherScraps](2)) to brew mead bases
* Build a **Fermenter** ([FineWood](30), [Bronze](5), [Resin](10)) to turn those bases into finished meads
* Use portals and start traveling towards the boss
* Craft [BronzeNails](80) — 4 bronze at the forge — and the first real boat, the **Karve**

### Bring fire, use mob vs mob

Almost everything takes Fire {modbox:Fire:VeryWeak} damage. Use your torch and camp fires to deal bonus damage.

Mobs will fight each other based on faction. [TrophySkeleton]@"Skeleton" are `undead` and attack `forest` faction.

[ArrowFire]@ costs [Wood](8), [Resin](8), and [Feathers](2) for twenty at an upgraded workbench — and Greydwarfs drop [Resin]@ constantly, so the forest pays for its own ammunition. Keep a stack for anything difficult.

A lit [Torch]@ in hand is enough to make ordinary Greydwarfs think twice.

### Resources

The variety of resources will quickly clog your inventory. You'll need to learn to **drop and/or ignore items** that aren't useful. For example, don't collect money **until you have portals**.

At home, **you need to organize your stuff** using wood chests for storage. Start with a wood/stone box and then have one or two boxes per biome.

Separate storage you **don't "long-press" for stuff you take with you** like food and ammo.

## Places

### Burial chambers

There are multiple entrance types, but they all have random corridors packed with Skeletons, and the occasional Ghost. Use the doors to manage the encounters. Prioritize destorying evil bone pile spawners. Beware of [TrophySkeleton]@"Skeleton" archers and parry your way thru. Run to the exit if injured.

If you have a [SledgeStagbreaker]+, you can hit skeletons behind doors.

Some rooms hold an Elder vegvisir. Don't get lost, use a system like always turn left.

### Troll caves

Look for a large cave mouth on a hillside. Roughly a third of the caves have a Troll outside the entrance. If a Troll is inside the cave, you'll also find treasure.

There are two Troll variants where one carries a log and the other throws boulders. They both try to smash you.

A Troll is 600 HP that one-shots early armor. It is weak to pierce {modbox:Pierce:Weak} so use your bow or spear. Dodge everything and learn his attack patterns. The payoff is [TrollHide]@ which can be crafted into useful light armor.

<img src="/img/guide/black-forest/troll-cave.webp" onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='/img/guide/placeholder.svg'" alt="Troll cave entrance in a hillside with the troll visible outside." style="width:100%;max-width:600px;border-radius:8px;display:block;margin:12px 0">

### Abandoned stone houses

**Break the barrels** outside for easy resources. Check inside and sometimes under the floor for chests. Don't skip the larger stone ruins, these **mansions** may contain [QueenBee]@ and [FineWood]@.

<img src="/img/guide/black-forest/stone-house.webp" onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='/img/guide/placeholder.svg'" alt="A large stone ruin mansion, with a smaller abandoned stone house behind it." style="width:100%;max-width:600px;border-radius:8px;display:block;margin:12px 0">

### Mining ores

**Mining is loud** so be prepared for mobs to agro from far away. **Mining from above does more damage** so stay on top and try to hit multiple fragments for max DPS.

**Metal cannot be teleported** so consider building a cart using [Wood](20) and [BronzeNails](10) to get bulk amounts home efficiently.

## Boss

This boss alter can spawn all across the main area of the world. The good news is that Elder vegvisirs are common and turn up in three places:

* On the **back of stone buildings** — check behind every ruin you pass
* Inside the large stone ruins, the **mansions**
* In rooms inside **burial chambers**

<img src="/img/guide/black-forest/elder-vegvisir.webp" onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='/img/guide/placeholder.svg'" alt="An Elder vegvisir glowing red on the back wall of a stone ruin." style="width:100%;max-width:600px;border-radius:8px;display:block;margin:12px 0">

### Defeating the boss

You need **[AncientSeed](3)** from either Greydwarf Brute drops or from destroying Greydwarf nests.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 2500 |
| Staggerable | No |
| Weaknesses | Fire {modbox:Fire:VeryWeak} |
| Immunities | Poison {modbox:Poison:Immune} · Spirit {modbox:Spirit:Immune} |

2500 HP and it cannot be staggered, so there is no parry-and-punish rhythm here. This is a damage race you win with fire and positioning.

### Boss attacks

* **Root shoot** — a ranged volley that erupts from the ground at your position. **This is what the stone pillars are for.** Break line of sight and they can't hit you.
* **Stomp** — a heavy area slam when you are close. Roll out of the ring.
* **Root summons** — roots grow quickly from the ground and try to hit you.

### Boss fight

**Use the pillars.** Peek out, shoot, step back behind cover. This is the whole strategy and it works from start to finish.

**Bring fire.** [ArrowFire]@ does double damage against it. A stack of 100 and a decent bow will allow you to keep your distance.

**Campfires work too.** You can pop mulitple fires under his feet for combined damage over time.

Drops the [CryptKey]@"Swamp Key" and the [TrophyTheElder]@.

### Before you move on

* Get a carrot farm running — [Cultivator]@ and [CarrotSeeds]@ pay forever
* Bank [FineWood]@ and [RoundLog]@ now that you can fell birch and oak
* Keep a stack of [ArrowFire]@ made — the resin is plenty
* Build portals with [GreydwarfEye]@ and [FineWood]@ so the next biome is not a walk
