## Alternative biomes

Some patches of Plains get a **variant** when the world is created. The game doesn't name them, so you tell them apart by how they look. One patch can have more than one variant, except the three marked below, which never overlap.

| Variant | What's different | Per world | From centre |
|---------|------------------|----------:|------------:|
| **Menhir** | Standing stones. After Bonemass, [TrophySkeleton]@"Skeletons" rise here at night. Also appears in the Meadows | 1–3 | 750 m |
| **Bones** | Giant bones, and [TrophySkeletonPoison]@"Rancid Remains" up to 3 at a time. Also appears in the Swamp and Mistlands | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Lox** † | Big [TrophyLox]@"Lox" herds, up to 15. No Fulings, Fuling camps or stone towers | 0–1 | 2,000 m |
| **Goblin** † | Extra Fuling camps and huts, with [TrophyGoblin]@"Fuling" war bands of 5–7. No Lox or Deathsquitos. Stars **twice as often** | 0–2 | 2,000 m |
| **Death** † | Tar pits everywhere and extra [TrophyDeathsquito]@"Deathsquitos". No Lox, Fulings, camps, towers or stone circles. Stars **twice as often** | 0–1 | 1,000 m |

† Only one of Lox, Goblin and Death can apply to a patch. Each one can also be missing from a world entirely.

* **Lox** plains are the safest place to tame Lox, with no Fulings to interrupt.
* **Death** plains are the best [Tar]@ farm, if you can survive the Deathsquitos.
