## Forge of Potential

Somewhere in the Mountains are the ruins of an ancient altar, the **Forge of Potential**. It lets you push gear **past its normal max level**. It's a gamble, though, and the gods don't refund losses.

### Finding one

* It only appears in the Mountains, never close to the world spawn.
* A world has up to 10 of them, spread far apart.
* Each one is a fixed ruin, so you can't build your own. Put a portal next to it.

### How refining works

Open the forge like a workbench and pick an item you're carrying. Each attempt costs the item's usual upgrade materials plus **one idol**. Then the gods decide:

| Result | Chance | What happens |
|--------|-------:|--------------|
| **Success** | 65% | The item goes up one level |
| **Shattered** | 35% | The item is **destroyed**. You get back about a third of the materials, but never the idol |

You get no partial result: the item either levels up or shatters. You can keep refining as long as you have idols and materials, but every attempt is the same 65/35 coin flip.

### Idols

Every item needs an idol from **its own biome**. Use **Protection** idols for armor, capes and shields, and **Battle** idols for weapons, bows and tools. A Wolf cape takes a Silver Protection Idol, for example.

|-----------|---------------|

| [Upgrader2Weapon]@ / [Upgrader2Armor]@ | Swamp | {biome:swamp}

Idols are loot. Look in treasure chests in dungeons, caves, ruins and shipwrecks in every biome. Later biomes also drop them from breakable pots and junk piles.

### Before you refine

* **Reloading won't save you.** The result is decided ahead of time and saved with your character. If your item shatters and you restore a backup, the same attempt shatters it again.
* **Only refine what you can afford to lose.** Have the materials ready to craft a replacement first.
* **Bring extra materials.** Every attempt costs a full upgrade's worth of materials, win or lose.

## Alternative biomes

Some patches of Mountain get a **variant** when the world is created. The game doesn't name them, so you tell them apart by how they look. A patch gets at most one of these three.

| Variant | What's different | Per world | From centre |
|---------|------------------|----------:|------------:|
| **Wolf** | More fir trees and fewer rocks. [TrophyWolf]@"Wolf" packs of 2–4, up to 8 at a time. No Drakes or Skeletons. Stars **twice as often** | 1–5 | 2,000 m |
| **Drake** | Rocky, with no fir trees. Extra [TrophyHatchling]@"Drakes", up to 4. No Wolves or Skeletons. Stars **twice as often** | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Fortress** | Crumbling stone walls and a ruined tower. [TrophySkeleton]@"Skeleton" groups of 3–6 and a [TrophySGolem]@"Stone Golem". Stars **three times as often** | 1–2 | 2,000 m |

* **Wolf** mountains are the place to farm wolf pelts and trophies, and to find a wolf to tame. Expect starred packs.
* **Fortress** mountains are the most dangerous patch in the biome. Don't go in without good armor.
