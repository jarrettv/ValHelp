## Jotun Invasions

At the bottom of every **Mörkhalla** dungeon sits a block of **Black Ice**. Break it and the game announces *"The Jotun Advance"*: a frozen invasion zone appears somewhere in the world and gets marked on your map. You need three of these to summon [Kall Fimbulbringer](/guides/enemies/deep-north).

### Where it lands

The spot is **random every time**. It doesn't depend on which dungeon you broke, and you can't predict it from the world seed. The game keeps rolling spots until it finds one that fits all of these:

* **Biome:** Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain or Plains. It never lands in the Mistlands, Ashlands, Deep North or the ocean.
* **Distance:** between 1,000 m and 12,000 m from the world centre. Anywhere in that ring is about equally likely.
* **Away from players:** more than 450 m from everyone online, so it never drops on top of you or a friend.
* **Away from other invasions:** at least 400 m from any invasion still running.
* **Ground:** dry land a little above the shoreline, not too steep.

If no spot fits, nothing happens apart from the announcement. At most **three** invasions can run at once. Breaking more Black Ice while three are active does nothing.

### What you'll find

* A **map marker** with a frozen circle 100–300 m across. The marker is temporary and goes away when the invasion ends.
* A **Black Ice Core** at the centre. While it stands, Jotun keep spawning nearby.
* Ice shelves on any water inside the circle, plus Deep North weather in the area.

Invasions never expire on their own, and they survive a restart. Break the core to end the invasion (*"The Jotun Retreat"*) and get one [HatefulBlood]@"Malicious Blood".

### Tips

* **Start all three before you travel.** Clear three Mörkhallas, then do the invasions in one trip with portal materials in your pack.
* **Farming the Jotun? Guard the core.** Meteors and enemy attacks can break it, and that ends the invasion.
