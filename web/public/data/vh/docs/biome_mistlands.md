## Alternative biomes

Some patches of Mistlands get a **variant** when the world is created. The game doesn't name them, so you tell them apart by how they look. One patch can have more than one variant.

| Variant | What's different | Per world | From centre |
|---------|------------------|----------:|------------:|
| **Rockless** | Fewer cliffs and rock spires, and a carpet of young Yggdrasil shoots | 2–5 | 2,000 m |
| **Trees** | Dense Yggdrasil shoots and roots, good for [YggdrasilWood]@ | 1–5 | 2,000 m |
| **Swords** | Giant swords and helmets sticking out of the ground | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Hare** | [TrophyHare]@"Hares" everywhere, up to 20 | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **BroodSwarm** | Swarms of 5–10 [Bestiary_SeekerBrood]@"Seeker Brood" at night | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Bones** | Giant bones, and [TrophySkeletonPoison]@"Rancid Remains" up to 3 at a time. Also appears in the Swamp and Plains | 1–3 | 2,000 m |

* **Hare** patches are the easy place to farm hare meat and trophies.
* **Trees** patches are an [YggdrasilWood]@ farm. Each shoot has a small chance to drop some.
