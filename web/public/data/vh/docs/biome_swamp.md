## Alternative biomes

Some patches of Swamp get a **variant** when the world is created. The game doesn't name them, so you tell them apart by how they look. One patch can have more than one variant.

| Variant | What's different | Per world | From centre |
|---------|------------------|----------:|------------:|
| **Hut** | Swamp huts on stilts, wooden walkways and log poles. Can't overlap a Bog | 1–3 | 1,500 m † |
| **Bog** | Dead firs, fallen logs and evil statues. Extra [TrophyAbomination]@"Abominations", up to 3 at a time | 1–5 | 1,500 m † |
| **Abomination** | Extra [TrophyAbomination]@"Abominations", up to 3 at a time | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Bat** | Swarms of [Bestiary_Bat]@"Bats" | 1–3 | 2,000 m |
| **Bones** | Giant bones, and [TrophySkeletonPoison]@"Rancid Remains" up to 3 at a time. Also appears in the Plains and Mistlands | 1–3 | 2,000 m |

† Swamps only exist 2–6 km from the centre, so in practice every variant starts at 2 km.

* **Bog** and **Abomination** swamps are where to farm Abominations. See the [Abomination page](/guides/enemies/swamp/TrophyAbomination) for spawn rates.
* **Hut** swamps are meant to have Wraiths haunting them at night, but a game bug stops those extra Wraiths from ever spawning.
